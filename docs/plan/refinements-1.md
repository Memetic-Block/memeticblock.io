# Memetic Block Website Refinements v2
**Date:** June 7, 2026
**Purpose:** This document outlines targeted modifications to the website *after* the previous update plan has already been implemented.

## Strategic Feedback from Strategist

We are currently over-indexing on "enterprise-grade distributed systems" language in a way that may scare away good smaller customers, web projects, SaaS products, and B2B opportunities.

**New Direction:**
- Show that we do high-quality, serious work (including enterprise-grade distributed systems)
- Do **not** heavily define ourselves by specific industries or only large customers on the About page
- Clearly communicate that our expertise applies to **web, internet infrastructure, SaaS, and B2B products** in addition to complex distributed systems
- Emphasize our actual work and quality level more than declarations of who we serve
- Keep "human-in-the-loop" and "agentic coding teams" but integrate them naturally

## Specific Modifications Needed

### 1. Homepage
- Revise the hero/main messaging to use the broader positioning
- Suggested core line:
  > "A software guild combining senior engineers with agentic coding teams under human-in-the-loop oversight. We deliver high-quality production software — from complex distributed systems to web applications, SaaS platforms, and >

- Strengthen showcase of work rather than just claims
- Ensure clear path to case studies and contact

### 2. About Page
- **Significantly reduce** any language that lists specific industries or implies we only work with large enterprises
- Add a balanced "What We Build" section that includes:
  - Enterprise-grade distributed systems
  - Web applications and internet infrastructure
  - B2B SaaS products
- Keep focus on **how we work** (senior engineers + agentic teams + human-in-the-loop) and the quality we bring to every project
- Make the page feel inclusive to both large and smaller customers

### 3. Case Studies Page
- Ensure it is highly visible and well-linked from homepage and About
- Consider adding 1–2 shorter examples that show web/SaaS/B2B work (even if smaller in scope) to demonstrate range
- Update Anyone Protocol entry to 7500+ relays with appropriate tone

### 4. Overall Tone & Positioning
- Shift emphasis from "we only do big infrastructure" → "we do serious, high-quality work across a range of technical challenges"
- Use "production-grade" and "high-quality engineering" frequently
- Keep "enterprise-grade" but use it as a *level of quality* rather than a market segment

### 5. Technical Checks
- Verify all pages (/about, /case-studies, /contact) are fully rendered and no content is hidden via Vue or CSS
- Ensure internal links between the four main pages are strong
